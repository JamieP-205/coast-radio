Help

How to listen

On this website
Press Listen live on the home page, or the button in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
On a phone or tablet
Open this website, or search for Coast Internet Radio in your radio app.
On a smart speaker
Ask it to "Play Coast Internet Radio".
In the car
Start Coast on your phone and connect your phone to the car over Bluetooth.

If the player won't work on your device, try the direct stream or Coast on Online Radio Box.

Questions

I pressed Listen live and nothing happened

Check your volume is turned up, then press Listen live again. If it still won't play, try the direct stream or Coast on Online Radio Box.

When is Jim on air?

Jim's live shows are listed on the show times page, in UK time. Music plays the rest of the time.

How do I request a song?

Fill in the request form, text Jim on 07935 889228, or email coastradio@hotmail.com.

Can I make the text bigger?

Yes. Use the A, A+ and A++ buttons at the very top of the page.

Privacy

This website doesn't use cookies, adverts or tracking. Your text size and dark mode choices are saved on your own device, along with a copy of the latest wording on the website so pages open quickly.

To show album covers, your browser asks Apple's iTunes search for each song's cover, so Apple receives those requests from your device. Requests and feedback you send are emailed to the station through Netlify, which hosts this website.
